Lendr Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Lendr is an app that lets college students rent everyday items to each other. This policy explains what information Lendr collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your information, and you can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, your school email address, and a password. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • Campus: we work out your school from your email address (for example, an @calvin.edu address puts you in the Calvin campus) and mark you as a verified student once you confirm that email.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to add one.
  • Listings: the items you list, including their title, category, description, daily price, deposit, the pickup spot you type in, and any photos you add.
  • Rentals: rental requests you send or receive, their dates, prices, and status.
  • Reviews: star ratings you leave after a rental.
  • Wanted posts: requests you post for items you are looking for.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not use your device's location (GPS). Pickup spots are only the text you type.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not process payments. Any money exchanged for a rental is handled directly between students, outside the app.
  • Lendr only reads the photos you pick yourself. It cannot see the rest of your photo library.

How your information is used

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show your listings, wanted posts, and profile to other students.
  • To handle rental requests between you and other students.
  • To show your stats on your profile, such as your rating, Lendr points, and earnings.
  • To send account emails, such as the link that confirms your email address.

What other students can see

Lendr is a marketplace, so some of your information is visible to other signed-in users: your name, profile photo, campus, verified-student badge, your listings and their photos, your wanted posts, and the ratings on your items. When you request or accept a rental, the other student can see the rental details. Your email address and password are never shown to other users.

Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Lendr's data is stored by Supabase, the service that hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in. Supabase processes this data only to run Lendr. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: go to Profile, then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, listings, rentals (including their reviews), wanted posts, and login. Deletion cannot be undone. Backups kept by our hosting provider may hold copies for a short time before being overwritten.

You can change your name and profile photo at any time from your Profile.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access rules in our database let each person change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Lendr is meant for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the app.
